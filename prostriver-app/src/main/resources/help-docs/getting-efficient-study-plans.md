# Getting Efficient Study Plans

ProStriver's AI study plan generator responds directly to what you tell it. A vague request gets a generic plan — a specific one gets a plan that's actually built around your situation. Here's what each field does and how to use it well.

## The Five Inputs

**Topic** — Be as specific as you comfortably can. "System Design" and "System Design: designing a URL shortener" will produce meaningfully different plans — the second is narrower and more actionable.

**Time Available** — This isn't just a label. Your entire time budget gets distributed across every topic in your plan, weighted by how difficult and important each one is. If you say "2 weeks," the plan is built to actually fit in 2 weeks. Be realistic here — overstating your available time produces a plan that's too shallow for the time you actually have, and understating it produces one that's rushed for no reason.

**Purpose** — This actively changes the *style* of your plan, not just its content:
- Preparing for an **interview** shifts the plan toward practical patterns, hands-on projects, and mock-practice style content.
- Preparing for an **exam** shifts it toward theory, formulas, and revision-focused content.
- Preparing for a **job** in general shifts it toward industry tools and resume-relevant skills.

Naming your actual goal clearly — rather than leaving it vague — is one of the highest-leverage things you can do to get a plan that's actually useful to you.

**Current Level** — Choose Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert honestly. Beginner plans start from fundamentals. Expert plans deliberately skip the basics and go straight to depth and optimization. Overstating your level means the plan assumes you already know things you might not — understating it means you'll sit through material you don't need.

**Notes** — This is your space for anything specific: "I already know the basics, skip them," "I prefer video resources over reading," "focus more on the practical side than theory," or any particular constraint you have. Whatever you write here is read as a direct, specific instruction — most people leave this blank, which means most people are leaving easy improvements on the table.

## What to Expect

- Generation happens in the background and takes a little time — this is normal, the system is calling an AI model to build a real, structured plan rather than pulling from a template.
- If your request isn't actually about studying, learning, or career/skill preparation, the generator intentionally returns an empty plan rather than making something up. This is expected behavior, not a bug — ProStriver is built specifically for learning plans.
- Every plan includes curated resources (a documentation link, one or two video resources, and sometimes an extra resource like a project or practice platform), common mistakes to watch for, and short actionable tips — not just a list of subtopics.

## After You Have a Plan

Use **Start Preparation** to mark it active, then check off each subtopic as you complete it. Your progress is tracked exactly — even accidental double-taps or having the plan open in two tabs won't throw off your completion count.
