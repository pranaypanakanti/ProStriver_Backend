# Getting Started with ProStriver

ProStriver helps you study with structure instead of relying on willpower alone. You track what you're learning, get scheduled reminders to revise it, and can generate a full AI study plan for any topic — broken into ordered subtopics you check off as you go.

## 1. Create Your Account

Sign up with your email and a password. ProStriver verifies every new account with a one-time password (OTP) sent to your inbox — check your email right after signing up and enter the code to activate your account. If the OTP expires or you don't receive it, you can request a new one from the signup screen.

## 2. Log In

Once verified, log in with your email and password. Your session stays active through a secure token that refreshes automatically in the background, so you won't be logged out mid-session — though for security, a session does eventually expire and you'll be asked to log in again after extended inactivity.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot Password** on the login screen to reset it via email — no need to contact support for this.

## 3. Add Your First Topic

From your dashboard, add a **Topic** — anything you're studying or want to revisit regularly. Once added, ProStriver automatically schedules spaced-repetition revisions for it, so you'll be reminded to review it at increasing intervals as you retain it better over time. You'll also start receiving a daily email digest summarizing what's due.

## 4. Generate Your First AI Study Plan

Beyond simple topic tracking, ProStriver can generate a full, structured study plan for any subject. Head to **Study Plan**, fill in what you're learning, how much time you have, why you're learning it, and your current level — then submit.

Generation takes a little time (it's calling an AI model in the background), so you'll see a "generating" state before your plan appears. Once it's ready, you'll get an ordered breakdown of topics and subtopics, each with resources, key concepts, and tips — and you can check off each subtopic as you complete it.

For a deeper guide on writing a better request to get a noticeably better plan, see **Getting Efficient Study Plans**.

## 5. Explore Everything Else

From here, **Features** covers everything else ProStriver does in full — including Challenges (streak-based accountability) and Analytics (a look at your progress over time).

If anything doesn't behave the way you expect, **Troubleshooting & Errors** covers what the common messages mean and what to do about them.
