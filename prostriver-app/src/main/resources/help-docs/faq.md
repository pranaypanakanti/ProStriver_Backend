# Frequently Asked Questions

**Is ProStriver free to use?**
Yes, ProStriver is free to use.

**Who built ProStriver?**
ProStriver is built by a small team. See **Team** for individual details, and **About & Vision** for the story behind the product.

**Is my data secure?**
ProStriver uses industry-standard authentication (JWT-based sessions with automatic token refresh) and never stores your password in plain text. Your account data is only ever accessible to you.

**Can I use ProStriver on my phone?**
Yes. ProStriver is a fully responsive web app and works well in any modern mobile browser — no separate app installation is required.

**How does the AI study plan generator work?**
You describe what you're learning, how much time you have, your goal, and your current level, and ProStriver generates a structured, personalized study plan broken into topics and subtopics. See **Getting Efficient Study Plans** for tips on getting the best results.

**Why does generating a study plan take a moment instead of being instant?**
Generation happens in the background because it involves a real AI model producing a structured, personalized plan rather than pulling from a static template. This also means submitting a request never leaves you waiting on a blocked loading screen — you can navigate away and check back.

**What is spaced repetition, and why does ProStriver use it?**
Spaced repetition schedules your revisions right before you're likely to start forgetting something, rather than on a fixed or random schedule. It's a well-established, evidence-based technique for long-term retention, and it's built into how ProStriver schedules topic revisions automatically.

**What are Lock-In Challenges?**
Optional streak-based accountability challenges you can opt into, evaluated automatically based on your real activity — a way to add extra motivation on top of your regular study tracking.

**I found a bug or have a suggestion — what do I do?**
Use the in-app **Feedback** form, or email **support@prostriver.me** directly.

**How do I delete my account or request my data?**
Contact **support@prostriver.me** and we'll help you directly.
