# About & Vision

## What ProStriver Is

ProStriver is a study platform built around one idea: consistent, structured revision beats last-minute cramming every time. Instead of relying on willpower to remember to study, ProStriver tracks what you're learning, schedules your revisions automatically using spaced repetition, and can generate a full, personalized AI study plan for any subject — broken into a clear, ordered path you actually follow.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to turn "I should study more" into a system you actually follow. That means:

- **Tracking your progress** so you always know exactly where you stand.
- **Scheduling your revisions automatically**, at the intervals that actually help long-term retention, rather than leaving it to memory or motivation.
- **Generating a study plan tailored to your goal** — whether that's an exam, a job interview, or picking up a new skill from scratch.

We believe the biggest gap in most people's learning isn't information — it's follow-through. ProStriver exists to close that gap.
