# Features

## Authentication & Account Security

- **Email + password signup** with OTP email verification before your account is active.
- **Secure sessions** using industry-standard token-based authentication, with automatic session refresh so you stay logged in without repeatedly re-entering your password.
- **Forgot password / reset password** via email, with no support ticket required.
- **Change password** from your profile once logged in.
- **Log out** ends your session immediately.

## Topics & Spaced Repetition

- **Add, edit, and archive topics** you're studying or want to revisit.
- **Automatic revision scheduling** — once a topic is added, ProStriver schedules future revisions for it using spaced repetition, so you review it right before you'd naturally start forgetting it, not on a random or fixed calendar.
- **Daily email digest** summarizing what's due for revision that day, sent automatically each morning.
- **Manual reminder patterns** — if you'd rather set your own custom revision schedule for a specific topic instead of the automatic one, you can define your own pattern of days.
- **Mark revisions complete** as you go, which keeps your schedule and streaks accurate.

## AI Study Plans

- **Generate a full study plan** for any topic by describing what you're learning, how much time you have, your purpose, and your current level.
- **Structured output** — every plan is broken into main topics, each with subtopics, estimated time, difficulty and importance ratings, curated resources (documentation, video, and an extra resource where relevant), common mistakes to avoid, and actionable tips.
- **Track subtopic completion** — check off each subtopic as you finish it. Your progress count updates immediately and accurately, even if you tap the same subtopic more than once or have it open in two tabs.
- **Start Preparation** — mark a plan as actively in progress once you begin working through it.
- **Generation runs in the background** — you submit your request and get a result shortly after, rather than waiting on a loading screen the whole time.

See **Getting Efficient Study Plans** for how to phrase your request to get meaningfully better output.

## Lock-In Challenges

- **Streak-based accountability challenges** you can opt into, evaluated automatically each day based on your actual activity.
- Multiple challenge lengths available, so you can commit to a shorter or longer accountability window depending on your goal.
- Your challenge status updates automatically — no manual check-ins required.

## Analytics

- **Daily progress tracking** — a running record of what you've completed each day.
- **Monthly summaries** — a higher-level view of your consistency and progress over the past month.

## Feedback

- A feedback page where you can write your thoughts and send them straight to the team via email — no account or backend submission involved, it simply opens your email client with your message pre-filled.

## Account & Profile

- View and manage your profile details.
- All of the above works cleanly on both desktop and mobile — the app is fully responsive.
